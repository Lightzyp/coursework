Step 1: Business Domain - Healthcare Clinic

Description: This clinic provides outpatient medical services, including consultations, treatment plans, and medication tracking. A database is essential to manage patient records, appointment schedules, and medical histories efficiently.

Core Service: Offering personalized healthcare, tracking patient progress, and ensuring accurate records for each patient and doctor interaction.

Step 2: Business Description and Key Processes

Core Services: Providing medical consultations, tracking treatment plans, and managing medication for various patients.

Key Business Processes and Rules

Patient Registration: Each patient is registered with personal details and a unique patient ID.

Appointments: Patients schedule appointments with specific doctors, including details like appointment date and time.

Consultations: Each appointment leads to a consultation record, tracking diagnoses, doctor notes, and recommended treatment plans.

Medication Tracking: The database includes medication schedules for patients, tracking prescribed medicines, dosages, and schedules.

Billing: Each consultation has an associated bill based on services rendered, which patients can pay overtime or upon each visit.
