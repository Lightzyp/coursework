In Chapter 2, we learn about research.  Think of a topic of psychology you would like to research.  Share which research method/design you would select to use for researching your topic.  Share your research topic, which design and reason for selecting research design.  (at least 4-5 sentences)
My research focuses on how mindfulness meditation helps college students feel less stressed. To manipulate the independent variable mindfulness meditation and see how it affects the dependent variable stress levels, I would employ an experimental research design. Random assignments would be used to place participants in either the control group or the meditation group. Because it offers causal evidence, this strategy is perfect for determining whether mindfulness meditation directly lowers stress.
