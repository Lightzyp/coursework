Private Browsing Lab

Lab Purpose (In Your Own Words)

The purpose of this lab was to compare anonymous browsing tools by using Tails with the Tor Browser and then compare that experience to using a web proxy. I tested how my visible IP address and geolocation changed when browsing through Tor, after generating a new Tor identity, and after using a proxy service. The goal was to understand how these tools protect privacy, but also to recognize that they do not guarantee complete anonymity.

Lab Procedures

For this lab I downloaded Tails ISO and set it up in VMWare as a new virtual machine using Debian 64 bit. I started Tails, Connected to Tor opened the tor Browser. I went to ipinfo.io to find the IP address and used a geolocation website ipinfo.io to see its location. Then on my normal browser, I checked my real IP address and compared the two.

Next I sued the “new identify “ feature in Tor to get a different IP and check its location again , I also visited websites like amazon and booking.com to see the location they showed finally I used a proxy website to check my Op address, and location ad compared its speed to Tor.
